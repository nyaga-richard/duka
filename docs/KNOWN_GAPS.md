# Known gaps and production release blockers

This is a working integrated application, not a promise that every ERP/compliance edge is complete. Read this alongside the requirement checklist. **No overall production approval has been issued.**

## External/host gates

- No public host/domain/DNS/Tunnel account deployment. Docker/Ubuntu templates exist and Caddy/Compose configuration validation ran; actual image builds/containers/systemd/filesystem/network/TLS/clock/load behavior still need host execution.
- No live Daraja merchant credentials, callbacks, provider statement reconciliation or B2C payout/refund validation. No certified/onboarded KRA OSCU/VSCU/approved fiscal gateway, taxpayer/device/item mappings or legal compliance validation.
- Local verified-TLS SMTP recovery worked, not a real external mailbox/DNS/bounce/alert setup.
- Encrypted DB+files restore has run locally, not a scheduled off-site separate-hardware disaster recovery operation with measured business RPO/RTO. Real destination, key custody, retention and alerting are absent.
- No remote Git push or independent security/accounting/legal acceptance. Source checkpoints are local until the operator supplies a remote/review/deployment process.

## Functional/financial limits requiring explicit decisions

- Outstanding legacy AR/AP AND party advance credits now have a guided reviewed carry-forward workflow: explicit debt/credit directions, real allocatable advance balances on posting, original-reference aging, real receipt/payment/credit allocation, immutable cutover and unused-batch correction with exact credit withdrawal. Cross-branch netting and guided correction after an opening item has settled (money moved or imported credit consumed) remain incomplete — those batches are intentionally reversal-proof history. Never turn a negative credit into positive debt or invent retail sales/GRNs to fill a gap. See OPENING_BALANCES.
- Fixed assets support opening-against-equity and paid-now (real tender, optional input VAT) acquisition, straight-line depreciation and final full disposal with exact accumulated/NBV snapshots, real proceeds tenders and DB-verified gain/loss posting; disposed registers stop depreciating and asset rows are update/delete-guarded. On-credit (accrued) capital acquisitions, partial disposals and asset domain corrections (re-registration of disposed assets) remain incomplete — book on-credit purchases through the reviewed manual-journal path until the AP-integrated variant is designed.
- Paid-expense actual refunds and actual refund repayments now have a dedicated immutable domain/UI/PDF workflow. Pure no-transfer erroneous-entry voids (original-date reversal, register restored, status 'void') and posted-expense account reclassification (capped partial moves, balanced journals) are implemented; voids/reclassifications/refunds are deliberately mutually exclusive per voucher and closed original periods refuse voids. Do not invent a refund or assume a GL-only reversal updates the register/cash records. Normal approvals/payments/atomic receipts are implemented.
- Source-based cash movements are not an independently classified statutory cash-flow statement. Period locks/reopening are not automatic statutory year-end closing/retained earnings. No multi-currency/FX/consolidation claim is made.
- Unconsumed successful external tenders/provider funds require explicit operator reconciliation; cancellation of an unposted checkout does not refund a real transfer or automatically book all provider suspense cases.
- Device authorization now has the dedicated `offline.manage` permission; during upgrade the legacy `sales.price_override` grant remains accepted as a fallback so existing role rows never lose access. Scope/active target/branch/warehouse/sales-create checks are enforced and regression tested.

## Offline/client limitations

- Warm-session constrained cash fallback only, not a complete cold-start offline app. No offline credit, prices/discount overrides, order conversion or fake bank/card/M-Pesa confirmation.
- New captures bind their original shift. Unposted legacy captures without a shift ID and replaced/closed-drawer conflicts require manager reconciliation. The UI blocks closure for its own unposted queue, but cannot attest to another device's unexported storage.
- Conflict review/re-entry, especially changed tax/price configurations, requires reconciliation of original cash and an authorized correction; not every case has a one-click guided resolution. Never edit JSON/change IDs to force acceptance.
- The isolated workspace viewer now supports explicit partitioned embedded cookies; production remains Lax/self-framed. Browser private drafts are not application-encrypted at rest. Use locked dedicated profiles/devices and disk protection. Broad storage-failure, device-loss and cross-browser fault testing is not exhaustive.
- Known uncertain online sales have durable recovery/cancellation with a permanent tombstone; generic non-sale uncertain commands still rely on unchanged-key retry or manager resource reconciliation. Authorization-scope changes intentionally block historical replay rather than disclose old privilege data; legacy responses have no invented scope.

## Security/quality gaps

- Exhaustive endpoint/CSV cost/field/positive-role authorization audit is now data-driven and registry-wide: tests/integration/privilege-matrix.test.ts sweeps all 155 route-registry entries — every authenticated route must 401 without a session (public surface is exactly the public-only mounts in app.ts: health, login, forgot/reset-password, password-complete, openapi.json, docs), every GET with a declared permission must 403 for a zero-permission role, every mutation must fail closed (4xx) for that role with fresh per-call sessions — POST /auth/logout is the documented session-control exception (any authenticated actor may end their own session, returns 200) — the strict-schema forgery sweep proves client-supplied company/creator/currency/branch fields are rejected on every mutation (endpoints without a declared schema still strict-parse unknown keys to 422), and the cross-tenant matrix holds company B to 403/404 for every concrete company A resource kind (product, customer, supplier, sale+receipt, expense+attachments, opening batch, journal, shift, print voucher, POS availability) plus random-uuid misses for the remaining parameterized reads. Per-permission BUSINESS outcome tests and curated environments remain, and full accessibility plus broader browser/mobile testing remain open.
- File validation is signatures/size/authorization, not full decoding/malware scanning or provenance attestation. Safe orphan maintenance is not automated; ambiguous COMMIT deliberately retains bytes. Crashes can leave private plaintext backup/restore staging; use encrypted/protected storage and reviewed cleanup.
- npm audit does not cover OS/images or logic; no penetration test, container vulnerability scan or actual-host load/failover/restore acceptance has run. Proxy/app log redaction is tested locally, but external proxy/CDN/SMTP/DB log privacy still needs deployment review.
- Rate limits/proxy topology must be validated for real shared-NAT/staff volume. No measured capacity/SLA, production monitoring destination or staffed incident process exists.

## Recently resolved (do not list as still missing)

M-Pesa STK initiation is idempotent: callers pass a stable client-generated key (the POS pins one per tender row and reuses it across retries/double taps), a partial unique index enforces one intent per (company,key), parameter mismatches fail closed with 409, and concurrent identical submissions serialize to exactly one provider push — re-dispatch is claim-locked and a no-op. Dispatches remain guarded against disabled/re-keyed company configuration after intent creation.

Company M-Pesa/eTIMS onboarding is self-service from the Integrations page: AES-256-GCM envelopes under the deployment key inside a FORCE-RLS table, write-only secrets with masked status, key-rotation fail-closed semantics (INTEGRATION_KEY_MISMATCH) and immediate enable/disable enforcement for in-flight dispatches and queued fiscal jobs. Validation (codes, HTTPS bases, sandbox exclusion in production) happens server-side. Live merchant/gateway certification remains an external gate listed above, not a code gap.

Paid supplier credits now have append-only same-supplier/branch allocation, actual refund and controlled reversal with concurrency/DB overdraw/date/scope guards; AP/aging/reconciliation include them. Parent-lock outstanding reads now refresh child snapshots. Central cash payout limits are enforced. Report defaults use company timezone. Count snapshots invalidate on warehouse/type changes and ignore late responses. Lost online checkout responses/429/reload and receipt failures preserve correct posting status; delayed cancelled packets are blocked in both service and DB. The POS live-price preview gates on the shared checkout schema before calling /pos/price, so unfinished decimal keystrokes (e.g. "5.") produce local fallback totals instead of 422 toast/console spam; stale-quote VALIDATION_ERRORs no longer surface, and the offline page suspends its device/sync queries while the browser reports itself offline. Decimal refine predicates in the shared schema are exception-safe (decCheck), so garbage that decimal.js throws on (5..5, 5e2, abc) is a 422 VALIDATION_ERROR, never a 500. Offline capture binds original shift and cannot convert an uncertain online request. See current test evidence/checkpoint rather than older failure logs.
